About Us

Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises

The Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME) is the apex body for the formulation and administration of rules, regulations and laws relating to micro, small and medium enterprises in India.

Our Vision

To promote growth and development of globally competitive Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises to enhance their contribution to economic prosperity and social well-being of the nation.

Our Mission

  • Promote and develop competitive MSMEs
  • Facilitate easy access to credit and capital markets
  • Enhance competitiveness of MSMEs through technology upgradation
  • Improve infrastructure facilities for MSMEs
  • Strengthen skill development and entrepreneurship training
  • Facilitate market access for MSMEs

Organizational Structure

The Ministry of MSME implements policies and programmes through its field organizations for the promotion of MSMEs. The following organizations are attached to the Ministry:

Office of the Development Commissioner (MSME)
Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC)
Coir Board
National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC)
National Institute for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (NI-MSME)
Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural Industrialization (MGIRI)
