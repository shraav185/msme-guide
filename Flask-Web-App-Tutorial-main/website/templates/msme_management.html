ABOUT MSME EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD

A First-of-its-Kind Initiative to Enhance Governance through Concurrent Monitoring and Pendency Reduction

The Ministry of MSME has launched an innovative Executive Dashboard, integrated into the Intra Gov Portal developed by NIC. This initiative utilizes real-time monitoring to ensure timely disposal of matters, significantly improving administrative efficiency.


The dashboard integrates over seven critical APIs, facilitating data exchange and monitoring across government ministries and departments. These include CPGRAMS, LIMBS, RTI, PIB, eOffice, APMS, and 10% Gross Budgetary Support. New API deployments are also underway, including CHAMPIONS and PFMS.


This solution extends beyond the Ministry of MSME to all government departments. By streamlining workflows and fostering transparency, it exemplifies the potential of digital transformation in governance.


Aligned with the government's commitment to support MSMEs—vital to India's economy, —this approach sets a new benchmark for leveraging technology to achieve 'minimum government, maximum governance,' positioning India at the forefront of digital governance and MSME support.
