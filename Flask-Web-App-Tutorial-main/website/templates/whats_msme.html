What's MSME?

Definition of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

In accordance with off with the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006 and subsequent revisions, MSMEs are classified as follows:

Classification Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment Annual Turnover
Micro Enterprise Investment does not exceed Rs. 1 crore Turnover does not exceed Rs. 5 crore
Small Enterprise Investment does not exceed Rs. 10 crore Turnover does not exceed Rs. 50 crore
Medium Enterprise Investment does not exceed Rs. 50 crore Turnover does not exceed Rs. 250 crore

Importance of MSMEs in Indian Economy

MSMEs play a crucial role in the growth of the Indian economy, contributing significantly to:

Manufacturing output
Employment generation
Exports
GDP contribution
Inclusive growth and development
Regional balance

Registration of MSMEs

The registration of MSMEs is now done through the Udyam Registration portal. It is a simple, paperless, and free registration process that provides enterprises with a permanent identification number.
